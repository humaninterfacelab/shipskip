# Color System

Use a cinematic red-toned palette built around deep reds, ember accents, and muted neutrals.

## Base Colors

- Background: near-black with warm charcoal undertones
- Elevated surfaces: deep oxblood / dark burgundy
- Surface overlays: smoky graphite with subtle red tint
- Borders: muted wine-gray with low contrast

## Typography

- Primary text: warm off-white / soft stone
- Secondary text: muted ash-gray with subtle red undertones
- Tertiary metadata: faded crimson-gray

## Accent Colors

Use accents sparingly and intentionally.

### Primary Accent

- ember red
- deep scarlet
- crimson glow

### Secondary Accent

- muted rose
- burnt coral
- soft ruby tones

### Highlight Accent

- warm amber
- copper
- low-saturation orange-gold

Avoid bright saturated red.

The palette should feel:

- premium
- cinematic
- atmospheric
- technical
- sophisticated
- slightly industrial
